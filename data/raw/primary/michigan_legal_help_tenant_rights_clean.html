Tenant Rights and Responsibilities

Publisher: Michigan Legal Help

Source: https://michiganlegalhelp.org/resources/housing/tenant-rights-and-responsibilities

Document type: Explanatory housing-law resource

Note: This is a concise, paraphrased reference to the source article, not a verbatim copy or a substitute for checking the current law.

Before You Rent

Review the lease term, rent, deposits, fees, and utility costs. Inspect the property, document existing problems, and understand the lease before signing.

Know Your Civil Rights

The article describes protected characteristics in housing and identifies discriminatory conduct, including refusal to rent, unequal terms, harassment, and certain failures to accommodate disability. It notes source-of-income protections effective April 2, 2025.

Before You Move In

Document the condition of the home with a move-in checklist, photographs or video, and signed copies for both tenant and landlord.

During Your Lease — Tenant Rights

The article discusses habitable conditions, repairs, rent escrow or repair-and-deduct options, quiet enjoyment, landlord entry, and conduct that interferes with a tenant's access or use of the home.

During Your Lease — Tenant Responsibilities

Tenants are expected to maintain the home, avoid damage, respect neighbors, pay rent and utilities, keep payment records, and follow enforceable lease terms.

Sharing, Changing, and Extending a Lease

The article explains shared lease responsibility, written mutually agreed changes, limited circumstances for unilateral changes, property sales during a lease, and possible renewal or month-to-month tenancy.

When You Move Out

Topics include early termination, subleasing, special lease-breaking protections, move-out condition, returning keys, forwarding mail, and providing a forwarding address for security-deposit matters.
