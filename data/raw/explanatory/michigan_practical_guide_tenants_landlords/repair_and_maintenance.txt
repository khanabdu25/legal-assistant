                                                         QUESTION 25: Are there circumstances in
                                                         which the covenants of habitability and
                                                         fitness do not apply?
                                                           Yes, MCL 554.139 provides some circumstances
                                                           under which the covenants of habitability and fitness
                                                           do not apply. For one, a landlord and tenant can
                                                           agree to modify the obligations of the covenants if the
                                                           current lease term is for at least one year. Additionally,
                                                           the covenants do not apply if the tenant is the cause
                                                           of the disrepair or violation of health and safety laws.
